Wall boundary lines are extruded as thin 8 cm visualization ribbons. Default wall height is 2.8 m and floor spacing is 4.0 m; neither is measured. Level 1 uses a local zero, not a surveyed elevation. Separated mode adds a 9 m display gap.

Floor plates are inferred outlines, not verified structural slabs. Door arcs and stairs remain plan linework; glazing is schematic. No roof, measured façade, stair flights, room labels, or navigation network is inferred.

NEW and EXISTING drawings may not represent one contemporaneous building condition. This is a spatial comparison and visualization, not a BIM or construction model.
